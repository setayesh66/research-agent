Aurora Jewelry Shop — Store Policies

Returns and Exchanges
We offer a 30-day return policy on all items in original condition. Custom engraved pieces and resized rings cannot be returned or exchanged. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of receiving the returned item.

Ring Sizing
Rings can be resized once for free within 60 days of purchase. Additional resizing after that period costs $25 per adjustment. Not all rings can be resized more than one size up or down depending on the design — halo and eternity band styles have limited sizing flexibility due to the stone setting.

Warranty
All fine jewelry purchases include a 1-year warranty covering manufacturing defects, loose stones, and clasp failure. The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, accidental damage, or loss.

Cleaning and Maintenance
We recommend professional cleaning every 6 months to maintain shine and check for loose settings. Customers who purchased from us receive complimentary cleaning for the lifetime of the piece — just bring it into the store.

Custom Orders
Custom design orders require a 50% non-refundable deposit and typically take 3-5 weeks to complete, depending on the complexity of the design and stone sourcing. We provide a 3D render for approval before production begins.

Shipping
Orders over $500 include free insured shipping. Orders under $500 have a flat $15 shipping fee. All jewelry ships in a discreet, unbranded box for security.

Materials We Work With
Aurora Jewelry works primarily in 14k and 18k gold (yellow, white, and rose), sterling silver, and platinum. Our stones include natural diamonds, sapphires, emeralds, rubies, and moonstones. We do not currently offer lab-grown diamonds but are evaluating adding this option in the future.
